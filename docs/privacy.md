# Skyscraper Town Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 26, 2026**　|　[中文](privacy-zh.md)

Skyscraper Town (摩天小镇, "the game") is developed and operated by **WANWU LAB (玩物软件)** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, how it is stored, and what rights you have.

In short: **your saves stay on your device (and, in the Google Play version, in your own Google account if you sign in to Play Games), you can play the whole game without signing in, and the game has no third-party analytics SDKs. The Google Play version has optional reward ads from Google AdMob: an ad plays only when you tap "Watch an ad" to get a bonus, and players who have bought anything never need to watch one (section 1.8). If you sign in to Play Games in the Google Play version, a few progress statistics are recorded through Play Games Services, which you can turn off in Settings. If you also turn on "Open to neighbors", other players can see your Play name, picture and tower (section 2).**

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 Data that stays on your device (we never receive it)

- Game saves, save thumbnails and settings (language, volume, which hints you have seen, and so on).
- The AI model address and key you enter yourself (see section 4).

Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes all of it.

**Cloud saves in the Google Play version.** In this version you can sign in to Google Play Games with the Google account on your device. Signing in is optional. If you do:

- Google gives the game your Play Games player name and player ID. The game uses them only to show who is signed in. They are not sent to us.
- The game stores a copy of your saves in **your own Google account** (Play Games "Saved Games") so you can get them back after changing phones or reinstalling. Google holds that copy. It is not on our servers and we cannot see it. You can turn off automatic uploads under Cloud Saves in the game.
- Achievements you earn in the game are recorded in the Play Games "Achievements" of your Google account (which ones and when), where you can see them in Play Games. After changing phones or reinstalling, the game uses that record to give your badges back when you sign in. Google holds this record too; it does not go through our servers.
- How Google handles this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). To delete the cloud copy, see [How to delete your account](delete-account.md).

### 1.2 Data sent automatically when the game starts

**The Google Play version sends none of the items in the table below.** It only downloads the balance configuration file described under the table.

In versions from other channels: after you tap "Agree and Continue" on first launch, the game sends the following to our server once each time it starts:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Device identifier | A string produced by a one-way hash (SHA-256) of the system identifier combined with this game's name. It is valid only for this game, cannot be reversed into the original system identifier, and cannot be used to recognize you in other apps. Used to count installs, manage how many devices an account can be signed in on, and prevent abuse. |
| System type | For example "android" or "windows". Used to investigate compatibility problems. |
| Game version | Used to investigate problems and understand which versions are in use. |

The game also downloads a balance configuration file from our server. As with any network request, the server sees your IP address and may record it in access logs, which are used only for security and troubleshooting.

### 1.3 Data you provide when you create or sign in to an account (optional)

**The Google Play version has no account with us.** It uses the Google Play Games sign-in described in section 1.1, and this section does not apply to it.

In versions from other channels, an account is not required. Only if you choose to register or sign in do we collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email address | Your account name, used for signing in and for verifying your identity when you make a request. |
| Password | Stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We cannot see your original password. |
| Signed-in devices | The device identifier, system type, version and sign-in time described above, so you can review and remove devices. |
| Entitlements | Whether you have unlocked paid features, when they expire, and redemption codes you have used. |

### 1.4 Purchases

- **Google Play version**: paid features are bought through Google Play in-app purchases. Google processes the payment. We never receive your card or payment account details, only whether the order succeeded.
- **Versions from other channels**: payment goes through a third-party payment platform, which processes the payment details. We record only the order number and whether payment arrived.

### 1.5 Feedback survey (optional)

The game includes a feedback survey. Filling it in is up to you. When you tap Send, the game sends the following to a survey we host on Google Forms:

- your answers (the options you picked and anything you typed);
- the game version, interface language, device operating system and model, owner level, and which unlocks you have bought.

It does not include your account, email address, player name, or any device identifier, so we cannot tell who you are from it. Please don't put personal information in the text you write. As with any web request, Google, as the provider of Google Forms, receives the request (including your IP address); see Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles it.

### 1.6 Progress statistics (Google Play version, only when signed in to Play Games; you can turn them off)

To see where players get stuck, **the Google Play version**, after you tap "Agree and Continue" and sign in to Google Play Games, records a few progress statistics using the "Events" feature of Google Play Games Services. **Turn off "Record progress stats" under Settings → Support and nothing more is recorded.** Nothing is recorded if you are not signed in to Play Games, or in versions from other channels.

Only these numbers are recorded: how many times you open the game, how many minutes you play in your tower, how many mini-games you start, finishing steps 5, 10 … 55 of the Owner's Path, and finishing the whole path.

Google keeps this data as part of Play Games Services, under your Google account, like the Play Games achievements described in section 1.1. **We only see totals across all players in Google Play Console**, not which player did what. These statistics do not go through our servers. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles them; to delete this game's data in Play Games, see [How to delete your account](delete-account.md).

### 1.7 What we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or the list of other apps on your device. Outside the Google Play version, the game does not use the Advertising ID. On Android the game requests only network-related permissions: Internet access and checking whether the network is connected. The Google Play version also uses Google Play Billing (for buying Time Cards).

### 1.8 Reward ads (Google Play version only)

The Google Play version offers optional reward ads: buttons such as "Watch an ad: claim again" or "Watch an ad: wait 15 min less". **An ad plays only when you tap one of these buttons.** There are no banners and no ads that pop up on their own, and each button has a daily limit. If you have bought anything in the game, these buttons give the reward without an ad.

The ads are served by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK), which we use as our ad provider. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects data such as your device's **Advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Google processes this under its own policies; see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We receive only aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown), not information about individual players.

**Your choices.** In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where consent is required, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change this at any time under Settings → Support → Ad privacy. On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in the device's Google settings. If you never tap an ad button, no ad is requested.

## 2. Neighbors: visiting other players' towers (Google Play version, only when signed in to Play Games)

"Neighbors" lets players signed in to Google Play Games visit each other's towers. It uses **Google Firebase** (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a Google service we use as our service provider, stored in the United States. Firebase uses your Play Games sign-in to give the game a Neighbors ID; your Google account email is not used.

**Your tower is uploaded only if you turn on "Open to neighbors"** (off by default; in Neighbors → Visitors, or Settings → Support). While it is on, the following is stored and **shown to other signed-in players**: your Play Games player name and profile picture (a small 64×64 copy), the layout of your current tower (floors, rooms, levels, colors, residents and staff as generated by the game), its star rating, height and owner level, and how many likes, flowers and visits it has received. It is updated about every 30 minutes while you play. Your saves, in-game money, the tower name and owner name you typed yourself, and device information are **not** uploaded.

**When you visit someone**, a short entry is placed in that player's visitor list: your Play Games player name, what you did (visited, liked, sent flowers, or left one of six preset notes), and when. There is no free-text chat. Visitor entries are deleted automatically after 14 days. Each action can be done once per day per tower.

**Playing games with neighbors.** Players who have turned on "Open to neighbors" can challenge each other to Gomoku, Reversi or Xiangqi and take turns. Each game stores both players' Neighbors IDs and Play Games player names, which game it is, the board, whose turn it is, the result and the time, and **only the two players can see it**. There is no free-text chat. Unaccepted challenges expire after 48 hours, declined or withdrawn ones are then deleted, and finished games are kept for up to 30 days.

**Report and block.** You can report a tower (inappropriate picture or name, or something else) and block its owner. A report stores who reported which tower, the reason and the time; we review reports in the Firebase console, and towers reported by three different players are hidden from browsing. Blocking hides each of you from the other; the list of players you block is stored with your public tower.

**Turning it off deletes it.** When you turn off "Open to neighbors", your public tower, its visitor list, the games you are in and your Neighbors ID are deleted right away (unfinished games count as a resignation). Entries you left in other players' visitor lists expire within 14 days. Reports you made are kept for up to 12 months for safety review.

## 3. What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not use your information for advertising or profiling ourselves. Reward ads in the Google Play version are served by Google AdMob as described in section 1.8.
- We do not include third-party analytics SDKs. (The Google Play version includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for the reward ads in section 1.8, and the Google Play Games Services SDK, used for sign-in, cloud saves, achievements, leaderboards and the progress statistics in section 1.6, and uses Google Firebase only for Neighbors in section 2.)

## 4. AI residents (optional, off by default)

In Settings you can enter the address and key of your own AI model service so residents can use it to generate dialogue. When enabled, the game sends the dialogue context (fictional in-game content such as a resident's name, job and situation) **directly from your device to the address you entered**. It does not pass through our servers and we do not receive it. How that service handles data depends on its own privacy policy. The address and key are stored only on your device.

## 5. Retention and storage

- Account data is kept until you ask us to delete your account.
- Device identifier records that were never linked to an account are kept for up to 24 months after the last launch.
- Server access logs are kept for up to 90 days.
- Feedback survey responses are kept in our Google account, used only to improve the game, and kept for up to 24 months.
- Neighbors data (section 2) is kept in Google Firebase while "Open to neighbors" is on and deleted when you turn it off; visitor entries expire after 14 days; finished games are deleted within 30 days; reports are kept for up to 12 months.
- Data is stored on servers we rent, and all transfers use HTTPS encryption.

## 6. Your rights

At any time you can:

- **Access and correct**: on the Account page in the game, view your email, change your password, and review or remove signed-in devices (Google Play version: the Cloud Saves page shows your sign-in status and the saves in the cloud).
- **Delete your account and related data**: see [How to delete your account](delete-account.md). We complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your identity.
- **Withdraw consent**: uninstalling the game stops all data collection.
- **Ask questions or complain**: contact us as described in section 9.

If you are in the EU, the UK, California or a similar jurisdiction, you also have the rights given by local law, such as access, portability, restriction and objection, which you can exercise through section 9. Our legal bases for processing are your consent (the device check-in) and performing our service agreement with you (accounts and paid features).

## 7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age set in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are a guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
